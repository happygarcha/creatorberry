## Deliberately NOT claimed
- That outdated equipment caused the stop (reporting points to a router during an upgrade; the equipment-age debate is a separate argument).
- Who is to blame (investigation ongoing; contractors and manufacturer dispute details).
- "Every flight" stopped (departures were halted; flights in the air continued). The title says "grounded", the narration says "nobody takes off".
