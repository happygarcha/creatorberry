# Episode 4 research notes: Panthalassa (researched 25 Nov 2026)

Company site panthalassa.com has only a home page and /join (no specs). Facts come from news and the company's May 2026 funding release as reported.

## Used in the video
- Portland, Oregon public benefit corporation, founded 2016 (GeekWire, Wikipedia). CEO Garth Sheldon-Coulson, co-founder Brian Moffat (GeekWire). About 120 employees (GeekWire).
- Name: Panthalassa is the ocean that surrounded the supercontinent Pangaea (general reference knowledge, not from company pages).
- Node: mostly steel, few moving parts; air-filled sphere on top, long central tube below; waves push water up the tube and it drains through turbines (Wikipedia, Interesting Engineering). About 85 m long (TechRadar, other press; Wikipedia's Ocean-2 figures are ~61 m hull + 9 m sphere, so the video says "reported").
- Not moored; steers by hull shape; CEO: "like a little Roomba, except it's enormous" (Wikipedia).
- Power used on board for AI inference; chips in sealed containers cooled by the ocean; results go to land by low-Earth-orbit satellite (Starlink named only by Wikipedia) (GeekWire, Interesting Engineering).
- Series B $140M led by Peter Thiel, announced 4 May 2026; about $210M raised in total per a company spokesperson (GeekWire). Valuation "close to $1 billion" per the Financial Times via Fortune/TechRadar (second-hand; video says "reports say").
- Prototypes Ocean-1, Ocean-2, Wavehopper tested 2021 and 2024; one source reports up to 50 kW for Ocean-2 in Puget Sound (single, unaudited). Ocean-3 pilot planned for the northern Pacific in 2026; commercial 2027 (all press).
- Vancouver, Washington factory at the historic Kaiser Shipyard site opened 6 Nov 2026 (OPB).
- Skeptic points: wave energy has a history of failures (CleanTechnica, TechSpot); no independently audited power, uptime or compute data (several outlets); remote repairs hard (Uptime Institute via Wikipedia); satellite latency may suit longer jobs (New Scientist via Wikipedia).

## Not claimed / left out
- Whether Ocean-3 has actually been deployed (not confirmed in any source found).
- Per-node cost ($1-1.5M, Panthalassa estimate via Latitude Media) and the $300M/50-jobs factory figures from low-quality sites (unverified; OPB's "more than $300 million this year" conflicts with $140M, so dropped).
- Storm survivability: no public data found.
