# AC water: "Don't drink it" (Line-Art Cutaway, 60 s, English)

## Three candidate structures
1. **Journey**: follow the water from room air to the glass (coil, pan, pipe, drip). Clear, but it ends on a shrug.
2. **Mystery from the symptom**: open on a clear drip, ask where it comes from, cut the AC open, trace the water, then dive into what it touches. Ends on the answer to the opening image.
3. **Before / after**: cut the same AC twice, clean coil versus grimy pan. Strong contrast, but it needs two full models and eats the runtime.

**Chosen: 1, with the turn from 2.** A journey along the one-colour water path (source to drip) with the turn placed where the accent reaches the pan and the film magnifies what the water touches. The hook is a single clear drip (thumbnail-sized); the ending returns to that drip, now at the end of a labelled path. Opening, signature shot (the lift-on-the-beat teardown of the pan, then the magnifier), camera route and score shape are my own for this topic.

## Logline and arc
A clear drip from an AC looks harmless, until the film cuts the unit open and follows the water through everything it touches.
Setup: the drip. Turn: the dive into the coil and pan (germs, metals). Ending: the drip again, now with a "do not drink" label, and a title block of three takeaways.

## Benchmark
Learn: textbook and patent cutaway drawings (line hierarchy, hatching, leaders), hardware explainers from knowledge channels (dense, exact). Don't take: any channel's characters, layouts, typefaces or music.

## Beat sheet and narration (about 2.5 words per second, at least 0.6 s between sentences)
- 0:00 "That clear water dripping from your air conditioner? Don't drink it."
- 0:06 "It is condensation. Warm, humid room air touches the cold coil, the moisture turns into drops, and they run into a pan and out the pipe."
- 0:16 "The same coil also pulls moisture out of your air, so the room gets dry, and dry air can irritate your skin, eyes and throat."
- 0:25 "In a closed bedroom, many room air conditioners just recirculate the air, so carbon dioxide builds up overnight. Studies link that to less deep sleep. Let some fresh air in."
- 0:37 "Now the catch. On its way out, the water touches the coil, the pan and the pipe. Germs can build up there, including, in some systems, the bacteria behind Legionnaires disease, and parts can add metals like copper, and lead from solder."
- 0:52 "So drain it, or water your plants. Don't drink it."

## Subtitle and title design
Bottom-left, one English line on a paper plate with a thin ink rule on its left; text writes on left to right and wipes off left to right; no source text anywhere on screen (permanent project rule). Title 54-64 px, one accent word ("DON'T DRINK IT"). Labels: anchor dot, leader, name in uppercase, at most one mono spec line.

## Accent
One accent colour (#0A84FF) only for the water path: drop, coil trace, pan, hose, the glass, one title word. Air and CO2 are graphite.
