# Facts used (sources are for the upload description only; none is shown on screen)
- Air-conditioner condensate forms where warm, humid air meets the cold evaporator coil and drains through a pan and pipe (basic HVAC).
- The coil also dehumidifies the air; about 30 to 50 % relative humidity is commonly given as a comfortable range (indoor air quality guidance).
- Singapore bedroom study (2004): CO2 was higher in air-conditioned than in naturally ventilated bedrooms. 2023 bedroom ventilation field study (PubMed 37142023): lower ventilation led to less deep sleep.
- Slate explainer, a university extension Q&A and EPA water-quality material: condensate is not treated as drinking water; drain pans and lines can harbour bacteria (Legionella outbreaks linked to cooling systems in hospitals, hotels and ships) and parts can add metals (coils often use lead-based solder; long copper runs raise copper).
