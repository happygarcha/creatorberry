# Kolkata's underwater metro (Line-Art Cutaway, about 74 s, Hindi)

## Three candidate structures
1. **Facts in a row**: bridge, tunnel, station, one label each. Accurate, but flat.
2. **Journey of the train**: follow one train from the deep Howrah Maidan station under the river to the far bank. Clear, but the "how was it built" part has no place.
3. **Section through the river, then dive into the three numbers** (520 m, 45 s, 33 m).

**Chosen: 3, built as one section of the real ground.** The camera always looks at the same cut through the Hooghly (hatched ground, accent-blue water), so the viewer keeps the picture of "water above, train below" and each number is a camera move on that one object. The hook is the Howrah-bridge-style truss above the cut, the sweep that slices the block open, and the train running through the bore.

## Logline and arc
A train runs under India's river. Setup: bridge, boats and a river on top, then the section opens to show the tunnel and the train. Turn: how the 520 m tunnel was dug (boring machine and concrete rings). Ending: back to the surface, boats above and the metro below.
