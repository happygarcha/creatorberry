# Facts used (verified against several sources; where sources disagree the film avoids the number)
- First underwater transport tunnel in India, under the Hooghly, part of the Kolkata East-West Metro (Line 2).
- River crossing about 520 m long; a train takes about 45 seconds to cross it.
- Howrah Maidan-Esplanade section inaugurated 6 March 2024; commercial service from 15 March 2024.
- Howrah Maidan: India's deepest metro station, about 33 m below the surface (sources say 30-34 m; the film says "लगभग तैंतीस मीटर"). 33 m = about an 11-storey building (3 m per storey).
- Tunnel dug with tunnel boring machines (earth-pressure-balance type) lining the tunnel with precast concrete rings.
- Not stated in the film because sources disagree: depth of the tunnel below the riverbed (13 m vs 16 m), TBM crossing time (36 vs 66 days).
- Drawing is schematic: vertical scale x5, bridge stylised, one bore shown.

Sources (description only): Wikipedia "East-West Metro Tunnel", ThePrint (KMRC obstacles; Modi inaugurates), Railway Technology, KMRC press brief 23 Jun 2017, Deccan Herald, PM India release.
