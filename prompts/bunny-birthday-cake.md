# Pip and the Wobbly Cake

**Format:** YouTube Short, 9:16 vertical, 60 seconds, silent-readable kids cartoon (music and sound effects only, no dialogue).
**Idea:** a little round bunny has to carry a wobbly cake to her friend's birthday party before the candles melt.

## Story

**Logline:** Pip the bunny bakes a tall strawberry cake for her best friend Bo's birthday, but the afternoon sun is hot and the candles are already drooping. She hurries across the meadow and over a log bridge, wobbling all the way. When the cake nearly tips, she shades the candles with her big ears and arrives just in time.

- **0:00-0:15 Setup:** Pip pops the finished cake out of her cozy burrow kitchen, tucks three candles in the top, and sets off. The sun climbs; the candles begin to bend.
- **0:15-0:30 Escalation:** Meadow path. The cake wobbles with every hop. She tiptoes past a line of marching ladybugs, a sunbeam bends the candles further.
- **0:30-0:45 Turn:** Log bridge over a stream. She slips, the cake spins on her paw, the candles are nearly flat. She covers the cake with her big floppy ears like a parasol.
- **0:45-1:00 Resolution:** She reaches the party clearing. Candles are bent but whole. Bo the bear gasps, Pip straightens a candle, they light them, everyone cheers.

Palette: butter yellow, meadow green, sky blue, strawberry pink and warm cream. Sunny afternoon light with soft shadows and a cheerful glow.
